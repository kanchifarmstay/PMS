Privacy Policy

What we collect when you enquire, book or pay — and what we do with it.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

The short version

We ask for your name, phone number and email so we can hold a room for you and talk to you about your stay. We use those details to run your booking and nothing else. There are no advertising trackers, no analytics scripts and no cookies on this website. We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing to people who have only enquired.

1. Who we are

Kanchi Farm Stay is a family-run farm stay near Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu, India. We operate this website, kanchifarmstay.com, and we decide how the personal information described here is used. In the language of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the Data Fiduciary and you are the Data Principal.

3. What we do not do

These are not aspirations. They describe how the site is actually built, and you can confirm most of them with your browser's developer tools:

  • This website sets no cookies, and stores nothing in your browser's local storage.
  • There is no Google Analytics, no Google Tag Manager, no Meta or Facebook pixel, and no other analytics or advertising tag anywhere on this site.
  • We do not track you across other websites, and we do not build advertising profiles.
  • We never sell, rent or trade your personal information.
  • We do not add enquirers to a mailing list, and we do not send promotional messages to people who have only asked a question.

Your browser does fetch our typefaces from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address in order to serve the font files. That is the only request this site makes to a third party on an ordinary page view.

4. Payments

Online payments are handled by Razorpay. When you pay, your card number, UPI ID, bank credentials and one-time passwords are entered into Razorpay's own secure checkout and go directly to Razorpay. They never reach our website and we never see or store them.

What we receive back and keep is the Razorpay order and payment reference, the amount, and whether the payment succeeded — enough to match a payment to your booking. Razorpay handles your payment data under its own privacy policy and under the rules the Reserve Bank of India sets for payment processors.

5. Who else sees your information

We share your details only with the services we need to run a booking:

Service What reaches them
Razorpay — payments Your name, email and phone number, and your payment details, which you give to them directly.
CallMeBot — the alert that tells us a booking has come in A message containing your name, phone number, email, room, dates and amount, delivered to the owner's WhatsApp so somebody knows to prepare for you.
Web2Phone — the contact form on our enquiry page Whatever you typed into that form, which it forwards to us.
Hostinger — web hosting Everything stored on the site sits on their servers, in India. They do not use it for their own purposes.
Google Fonts Your IP address, when your browser fetches our typefaces.

Beyond these, we disclose personal information only where the law requires it — for example a lawful request from a government authority, or the guest records a property is obliged to keep — or where it is genuinely necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.

6. If you booked through Booking.com or Airbnb

We also list our rooms on Booking.com and Airbnb. If you booked there, that platform collected your information under its privacy policy and passed us the part we need to host you — typically your name, your dates and a contact channel. This policy covers what we do with those details once we have them; it does not cover what the platform itself does, so please read theirs too.

7. Our calendar sharing keeps you out of it

To stop the same room being sold twice, we publish an availability calendar that Booking.com and Airbnb read. That calendar contains only dates, marked "Unavailable". No guest name, phone number, email address or amount is ever included in it.

8. Where your information is kept, and for how long

Booking records are stored in a database on our hosting account, held on servers in India and placed outside the public area of the website so it cannot be downloaded from the web. Administrative access is password protected.

  • Bookings and payment records are kept for eight years after the financial year of your stay, because Indian tax and accounting law requires a business to be able to produce its records for that long.
  • Enquiries that never became a booking are kept for up to twelve months, then deleted.
  • Web server logs are kept by our hosting provider on their own short rolling schedule.

9. Keeping it safe

The site is served over HTTPS, so what you type reaches us encrypted. Passwords, keys and the booking database are stored outside the public web folder and are blocked from being served by the web server. We keep the number of people who can see guest records to the family members who run the property.

No system is perfectly secure, and we would rather say so than promise otherwise. If a breach ever affected your information, we would tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

10. Children

This website is meant for adults making a booking. We ask how many children are in your party so we can work out the room and the price — we do not ask for a child's name, age or any other detail, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from anyone under 18. The adult who makes the booking provides whatever we need on behalf of the party. If you believe a child has given us personal information directly, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • Show you what personal information of yours we hold, and who we have shared it with.
  • Correct or complete anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Erase your information, once we no longer need it for your stay or for the accounting records the law makes us keep.
  • Withdraw your consent to being contacted. Do note that if you withdraw it while a booking is still ahead of you, we may have no way to reach you about your own stay.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.
  • Complain if you are unhappy with how we answered.

Write to ops@kanchifarmstay.com and we will reply within 30 days. There is no charge. We may ask you to confirm something only the real you would know — usually your booking reference — before we hand over or change anything, because handing a guest's details to the wrong person is the one mistake we most want to avoid.

If we have not resolved your complaint, you may escalate it to the Data Protection Board of India.

12. Changes to this policy

If we add a service that handles your information differently, we will update this page and change the date at the top. The date shown there is always the date the policy last changed.
